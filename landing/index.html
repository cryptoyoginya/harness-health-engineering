The problem

Every tracker optimised a number. Your life fell out of frame.

Recovery. HRV. Steps. Somewhere the metric became the point — while whether you felt good, did meaningful work, or saw people you love quietly stopped counting. That's Goodhart's law wearing a fitness band: when a measure becomes the target, it stops measuring anything that matters.

The shift

So we deleted the dashboard and kept one question.Is my life actually better?

Everything else — sleep, HRV, supplements, training load — is demoted to what it really is: an instrument in service of that answer. The system will tell you your body looks great this week and your life doesn't — then help you fix the right thing.

The science

Personal science, not correlations.

Correlations are guesses. We borrow the method clinical medicine uses to decide whether something works for one specific person — the n-of-1 trial. Pre-registered, controlled, judged against a line drawn before the data arrives.

The engineering

Local-first. No servers. No surveillance.

Your entire life is a folder of plain Markdown you own — searchable, versioned, yours to delete. The intelligence runs on the machine in front of you.

On-device voice
Whisper (ONNX) transcribes voice notes locally, decoded by a prebuilt ffmpeg — audio never leaves the device.
On-device vision
CLIP zero-shot classification routes every photo into the right diary, on the machine, in milliseconds.
Hybrid RAG
multilingual-e5 embeddings + sqlite-vec + FTS5 BM25, fused with reciprocal-rank — your history, retrieved with citations.
Agent surface
An MCP server exposes search, synthesis, and backlinks to any client — the model reasons over your pyramid, never raw guesses.
Knowledge pyramid
Layered Markdown 00→06 with frontmatter contracts and evidence labels — fact, inference, assumption, separated by design.
Quietly automatic
Race-safe Whoop OAuth, a morning brief via launchd, and a kb-doctor health-check keep it honest, hands-off.
