The problem

Every tracker optimised a number. Your life fell out of frame.

Goodhart’s law, wearing a fitness band: when a measure becomes the target, it stops measuring anything that matters.

One metric. Is my life actually better?

Everything else — sleep, HRV, supplements — is demoted to an instrument in service of that answer. And every part of your life orbits it:

Body × work × people × mind — all in service of one question

The engineering

Local-first. No servers. No surveillance.

Your whole life is a folder of plain Markdown you own — a layered pyramid where evidence flows up and nothing is asserted without a trail.

On-device voice
Whisper (ONNX) transcribes locally — audio never leaves the device.
On-device vision
CLIP zero-shot routes every photo into the right diary, in milliseconds.
Hybrid RAG
multilingual-e5 + sqlite-vec + FTS5, fused with RRF — your history, cited.
Agent surface
An MCP server exposes search & synthesis to any client — reasoning over the pyramid, not guesses.
Evidence labels
Every claim is fact / inference / assumption with a source — observation split from interpretation.
Quietly automatic
Race-safe OAuth, a morning brief via launchd, a kb-doctor health-check.
